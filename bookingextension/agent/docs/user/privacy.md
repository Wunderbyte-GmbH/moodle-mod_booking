# Privacy

The Booking Assistant is careful with personal data. It only ever shows you what you're already allowed to see, and it masks personal details in the saved conversation. This page explains what that means for you in practice.

## It only surfaces what you're already allowed to see

The assistant acts with **your** Moodle permissions — nothing more. When you ask it about users, courses, grades, enrolments, or anything else, it can only return information your own account is allowed to access.

So:

- If you can't see a user's grades in Moodle, the assistant can't show them to you either.
- If you have no access to a course, the assistant won't reveal its contents.
- If a report is restricted to managers and you're not one, you won't get it through the assistant.

The assistant isn't a back door. It's a faster way to reach the same information you're already entitled to — and only that.

## It won't expose contact details of users you have no relationship with

The assistant won't hand over personal contact details — like email addresses — for people you have no teaching or management relationship with. If you ask about someone who isn't in your courses or under your responsibility, it won't surface their private details.

When you do work with someone — for example a student enrolled in your course — the assistant can help you look up their profile, roles, grades, completion, and enrolment status, because those are things your role already lets you see.

## Personal data is masked in the saved conversation

Your conversation is kept so you can refer back to earlier messages and so the assistant can remember the context of what you were doing. To protect privacy, **personal data in the saved conversation is masked.** Names and other personal identifiers are hidden in the stored record rather than written out in the clear.

This doesn't get in your way. During your conversation, you still see the real names and details you're entitled to see, and you can keep referring to "that student" or "the user we just looked at" — the assistant follows along. The masking applies to what's kept behind the scenes, keeping personal data out of the stored history.

## What this looks like in practice

- **Looking up someone in your course is fine.** "Show me Maria Sanchez's completion in this course" works, because Maria is your student and completion is something you can see.
- **Diagnosing a problem is fine.** "Why didn't this student get the confirmation email?" works — it's about a user you're responsible for, and you're allowed to investigate.
- **Fishing for strangers' details won't work.** Asking for the email address of someone outside your courses won't return their private contact information.
- **Restricted reports stay restricted.** If a report needs permissions you don't have, you'll be told you can't access it rather than shown the data.

## You stay in control

Because the assistant mirrors your permissions and masks personal data in its saved record, you can use it for everyday tasks — looking up enrolments, checking grades, diagnosing why a booking didn't work — without worrying that it's exposing data you shouldn't see or storing personal details in the clear.
