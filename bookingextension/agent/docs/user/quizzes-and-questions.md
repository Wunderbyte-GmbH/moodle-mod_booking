# Quizzes and quiz questions

The wizard can add a **quiz** to a course, **update** an existing quiz, and **generate quiz questions** straight into the course question bank or into a quiz. You ask in plain language. Questions can come from text you type in the chat, an attached PDF, or the PDF files already in the course.

Adding or changing a quiz, and importing questions, are changes — so the wizard shows you what it will do and waits for your confirmation first. Nothing is saved until you say yes.

## Adding a quiz

Tell the wizard the name of the quiz and, if you like, which section of the course it should go in.

Example requests:

- "Add a quiz called Week 1 review to this course."
- "Create a quiz named Final assessment in the Assessment section."
- "Add a quiz called Practice test at the top of the course."
- "Make a new quiz titled Safety knowledge check in week 3."
- "Create a quiz Week 2 test with 10 questions from the course PDFs."
- "Create a quiz with 5 random questions from the category Chapter 1."

A quiz can be created empty, or straight away with questions: newly generated ones (from text you give, an attached PDF or the course PDFs), specific existing questions, or random questions from a question-bank category. If you want questions but don't say from where, the wizard asks.

The wizard shows you the quiz it will create — the name, the section and the questions it will add — and waits for your yes.

## Updating a quiz

Name the quiz and tell the wizard what you'd like to change.

Example requests:

- "Rename the Week 1 review quiz to Week 1 recap."
- "Hide the practice quiz."
- "Change the description of the Final assessment."
- "Add 5 questions about fire exits to the Safety knowledge check."
- "Add 10 questions from the course PDFs to Week 2 test."

As with any change, the wizard shows you what it will set and asks you to confirm before applying it.

## Generating questions for the question bank

You can turn material into quiz questions. Tell the wizard how many questions you want, what kind, and where they should come from: facts or a topic you write in the chat, a PDF you attach (see [attachments](attachments.md)), or the PDF files already in the course. You can also dictate a question and its answer. The wizard writes the questions and **imports them into the course question bank** for you, ready to use in a quiz.

The question types are **multiple choice**, **true/false** and **short answer**, and one request makes at most **50** questions. If you don't say how many, the wizard asks.

Example requests:

- "Generate 10 multiple-choice questions from this PDF."
- "Make 5 questions from the attached document for the question bank."
- "Create 8 multiple-choice questions based on this handout."
- "Generate 15 questions from this PDF about the safety procedures."
- "Turn the attached reading into 6 quiz questions."
- "Make 8 true/false questions from the PDFs in this course."
- "Create 5 short-answer questions about the water cycle."

> **You:** *(attaches lecture-notes.pdf)* Generate 10 multiple-choice questions from this PDF.
>
> **Wizard:** I've drafted 10 multiple-choice questions from *lecture-notes.pdf*. Here's a preview of the first few:
> 1. Which of the following best describes…
> 2. What is the main purpose of…
>
> Import all 10 into the question bank for *Workplace Safety*?
>
> **You:** Yes.
>
> **Wizard:** Done — 10 questions are now in the course question bank.

The questions go into the **course question bank**, so you can review, edit, and add them to any quiz in that course afterwards. The wizard shows you a preview before importing, and you confirm before anything is saved. If you'd like the questions placed in a particular category of the question bank, just say which one — and if it's not clear, the wizard asks.

## Putting it together

You can do it step by step — generate questions into the question bank, then add a quiz — or in one go. Ask it to create the quiz with questions straight away ("Create a quiz Week 2 test with 10 questions from the course PDFs"), or to add questions to an existing quiz ("Add 10 questions from this PDF to Week 2 test").

## Which course it works in

The quiz is added to, and the questions are imported into, the course you're currently in. If it isn't clear which course you mean, name it — "add a quiz to the Photography course."

## Permissions

The wizard acts with **your** Moodle permissions. If you're allowed to add a quiz and manage the question bank in this course, it can do it for you. If you're not, it tells you instead of changing anything.

## Tips

- Say how many questions you want and which kind ("10 multiple-choice questions") — multiple choice, true/false and short answer are possible.
- If a PDF is long, the questions cover the material you point the wizard at — you can say "focus on the chapter about first aid."
- You always get a preview of the questions before they're imported, so you can say "no" or ask for changes first. See [confirmations and safety](confirmations-and-safety.md).
- To see what's already in a course before adding a quiz, ask for an overview — see [getting a course overview](course-overview.md).
