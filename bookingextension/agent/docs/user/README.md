# The Booking Wizard — User guide

The Booking Wizard is an AI helper inside Moodle. You tell it, in your own words and your
own language, what you want to do with your bookings and courses — and it does it for you, or
answers your question. You don't need to learn any commands.

Managers open it from the **wand icon** in the top navigation bar; teachers open it from inside a
Booking activity. Then type your request into the chat box. See [Getting started](getting-started.md)
for who sees which entry point.

> Before it changes anything, the wizard shows you a preview and waits for your **OK** (the
> exceptions are listed under [Confirmations and safety](confirmations-and-safety.md)).
> It always works with **your** Moodle permissions, and it replies in **your** language.

Many examples in this guide are about booking options. Those parts need the Booking plugin
(mod_booking); they are marked *(requires mod_booking)* where they appear in a general chapter.
Which tasks are available on your site also depends on what your administrator has switched on.
