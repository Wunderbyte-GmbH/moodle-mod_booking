# The Booking Assistant — User guide

The Booking Assistant is an AI helper inside Moodle. You tell it, in your own words and your
own language, what you want to do with your bookings and courses — and it does it for you, or
answers your question. You don't need to learn any commands.

Open it from the **wand icon** in the top navigation bar, or from a Booking activity, and type
your request into the chat box.

> Before it changes anything, the assistant shows you a preview and waits for your **OK**.
> It always works with **your** Moodle permissions, and it replies in **your** language.
