# Example requests

You don't have to phrase things exactly like this — the assistant understands free text in any
language. These show the range of what you can ask. Each group links to a page with more.

## Creating and editing bookings

- "Create a new booking option called *Intro to First Aid* on 5 March at 14:00, 12 places."
- "Add a course on Mondays 09:00–11:00 for the next six weeks."
- "Set the price of *Intro to First Aid* to 20 €, students 10 €."
- "Change the teacher of the Tuesday session to Maria Huber."
- "Add a header image to this option." *(then attach the image)*

→ [Create](booking-options-create.md) · [Edit](booking-options-edit.md) ·
[Kinds of option](booking-options-types.md) · [Prices & bulk](booking-options-prices-and-bulk.md)

## Tips

- **Be specific about which option/activity/person you mean** — by name. If several match, the
  assistant asks you to pick. See [Choosing the right item](choosing-the-right-item.md).
- **Attach files** (image, PDF) when the task needs them; the assistant tells you when.
- **You can say "no"** at the confirmation step, or refine your request and try again.
