# FAQ

## Does it change things without asking?

No. Before any change — creating or editing an option, booking a person, adding an activity,
enrolling someone — the wizard shows you a preview and waits for your confirmation. Only
read-only questions are answered immediately. Two exceptions: a preference you ask it to
remember is saved right away, and if your site grants you the permission to confirm for a few
minutes at once, later changes in that activity run without a separate confirmation. See
[Confirmations and safety](confirmations-and-safety.md).

## What is it allowed to do?

Exactly what **you** are allowed to do. It acts with your permissions in the current course or
activity — it cannot do anything you could not do yourself in Moodle. Some actions need
particular rights: enrolment needs the right to enrol users manually in the course, and creating
courses and the Report Builder tasks are for managers by default. Tasks that change data are
also off until your administrator enables them. If something isn't available to you, the
wizard tells you.

## In which language does it answer?

In the language you write in. Ask in German and you get German; ask in English and you get
English. A new request in another language switches the conversation; a short answer such as
"yes" keeps the current language. Buttons and preview labels follow your Moodle interface
language. See [Languages](languages.md).

## Can it see other people's personal data?

Only what you are already entitled to see. It will not expose the contact details of users you
have no relationship with. Depending on your site's privacy mode, names and e-mail addresses are
replaced by placeholders before your messages reach the AI provider. See [Privacy](privacy.md).

## It picked the wrong option / course / person. What now?

Name the item more specifically (its exact title, the person's full name). If several match,
the wizard asks you to choose rather than guessing. You can also cancel at the confirmation
step and rephrase. See [Choosing the right item](choosing-the-right-item.md).

## Can it delete an option or cancel someone's booking? *(requires mod_booking)*

No. It can **create** and **edit** options and **book** people, but it has no action to delete
a booking option or to cancel a booking on someone's behalf. It can, however, *diagnose* why a
person can't cancel.

## Can I do several things in one request?

Yes. For a multi-part request it works through the steps in order and confirms each change as
it goes. See [Multi-step requests and limits](multi-step-and-limits.md).

## Can it remember my preferences?

Yes — ask it to remember something ("always default to 20 places") and to forget it later.
These notes are kept for your account only. See [Remembering and history](remembering-and-history.md).

## How do I attach a file?

Attach it in the chat with your request: an image (for example for a booking option's header)
or a PDF (for example to generate questions from). When a task needs a file, the wizard prompts
you. See [Attachments](attachments.md).

## Something went wrong / it couldn't do it

It tells you what went wrong in plain language. Common reasons: you lack the permission, a
required detail was missing or ambiguous, or a file was expected. Adjust your request and try
again — see [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).

## Can I ask it how a feature works?

Yes, if your administrator has switched this on. Ask "how does X work?" and it searches this
guide and the Booking manual and gives a short answer with the relevant passage; ask it to read
more for the full detail. See [Getting help](getting-help.md).
